\Title{时变碳强度下多车场混合车队动态协同配送模型与算法}
\Author{周雷习书, 干宏程, 邱莹莹}{上海理工大学 管理学院, 上海 200093}

\Abstract{%
多车场协同与车队电动化可降低区域配送成本和排放,
但充电时刻、参与条件和订单变化后的车辆状态决定方案能否执行.
针对路径、充电、协同与动态调整相互割裂的问题,
构建时变碳强度下多车场混合车队动态协同路径模型,
以配送趟—实体车排班两层结构描述有限车队、多趟衔接、趟间充电、
成员参与和滚动状态继承, 并在固定排班内识别充电择时减排.
设计两阶段多视角混合遗传搜索—限时MIP路线池重组算法,
由三种代理视角生成候选, 经完整模型和独立检查保证解可执行.
采用公开标准算例和中国三大城市群81个构造算例验证.

\ETitle{Model and algorithm for dynamic collaborative delivery with a
multi-depot mixed fleet under time-varying carbon intensity}
\EAuthor{ZHOU Leixishu, GAN Hongcheng, QIU Yingying}{Business School,
University of Shanghai for Science and Technology, Shanghai 200093, China}
\EAbstract{%
Multi-depot collaboration and fleet electrification can reduce delivery
costs and emissions, but charging time, participation, and vehicle states
after order changes determine whether a plan is executable. We formulate
a dynamic collaborative routing model for a multi-depot mixed fleet under
time-varying grid carbon intensity. A two-layer trip--vehicle schedule
represents finite fleets, multi-trip connections, inter-trip charging,
participation, and rolling state inheritance. A two-stage Multi-View
Hybrid Genetic Search generates three proxy archives and uses full-model
adjudication and time-limited MIP route-pool recombination. Public
benchmarks and 81 constructed instances from three Chinese urban
agglomerations are used for evaluation.
